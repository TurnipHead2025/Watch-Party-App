---
Title: 13 - Wait for It
Set Layout: Total open, bare stage floor. The upper scaffolding is sparsely populated by ensemble members who look down like a silent, judging gallery.
Lighting: Saturated, moody deep blue and indigo wash. A single, crisp white spotlight tracks Burr's movements across the stage.
Blocking Elements:
- Aaron Burr is the absolute visual anchor, standing downstage center and delivering his lines with restrained, highly controlled physical movement.
- The ensemble moves around him on the outer turntable ring in an incredibly slow, fluid, hypnotic contemporary dance pattern, acting as the physical manifestation of his thoughts and the passing of time.
- The inner turntable remains locked under Burr's feet, visually representing his choice to stand still, wait, and remain perfectly centered while the rest of the world spins chaotically around him.
---
