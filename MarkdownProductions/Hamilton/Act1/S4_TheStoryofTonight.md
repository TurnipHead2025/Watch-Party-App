---
Title: 04 - The Story of Tonight
Set Layout: A small, makeshift tavern setting. A single heavy wooden table and four mismatched wooden chairs are placed downstage center.
Lighting: A warm, rich golden-amber wash floods the center stage, simulating candlelight and a hearth, while the outer perimeter remains in a soft twilight blue.
Blocking Elements:
- Hamilton, Laurens, Lafayette, and Mulligan gather tightly around the table, raising tankards and slinging their arms over each other's shoulders.
- The movement is fluid, warm, and natural. Characters step onto chairs or lean heavily on the table as their excitement builds.
- The inner turntable rotates at a very slow, subtle crawl, gently showcasing the group from different angles to the audience as they pledge their friendship.
- The ensemble is absent from the stage floor, leaving the focus entirely on the core four.
