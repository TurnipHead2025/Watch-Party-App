Title: 06 - Farmer Refuted
Set Layout: A public town square. A small, elevated wooden soapbox or podium is placed stage right for Samuel Seabury.
Lighting: Cool, functional morning daylight. Two distinct white spotlights divide the stage: one on Seabury's podium and one on Hamilton downstage center.
Blocking Elements:
- Samuel Seabury stands elevated on his podium, delivering his loyalist speech with rigid, old-fashioned, repetitive hand gestures.
- Hamilton stands directly on the stage floor, initially ignoring Seabury before stepping up to stand right next to his podium, invading his physical space.
- As Hamilton begins to mock Seabury's lines, he weaves circles around the podium while Seabury tries desperately to maintain his straight-ahead focus.
- The ensemble acts as a divided crowd on the street, reacting facially to the argument but remaining mostly static until the sudden military interruption at the end.
