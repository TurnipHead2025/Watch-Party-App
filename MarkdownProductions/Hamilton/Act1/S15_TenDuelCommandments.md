Title: 15 - Ten Duel Commandments
Set Layout: A cold, isolated, clearing in the woods at dawn (Weehawken, New Jersey). The stage floor is completely cleared of all furniture and props.
Lighting: Starlight fading into early morning. A stark, cold steel-blue wash with a sharp, linear white spotlight cutting directly across the stage to form a physical dueling line.
Blocking Elements:
- Laurens and Lee stand back-to-back downstage center, perfectly aligned with the grid lines of the stage floor boards.
- Hamilton and Burr step out to opposite sides of the stage perimeter, acting as the seconds and mirroring each other's precise, rigid military movements.
- The outer turntable spins in sharp, mechanical, ten-second increments, moving the ensemble members in tight, staccato, clock-like choreography around the duelists to visually count down the rules.
- On the final count, the turntables lock instantly, leaving the two shooters frozen in profile facing each other.
