Set Layout: High-speed transition from the dark military camp to an elegant, high-society ballroom.

Blocking Elements:

- The outer turntable spins rapidly clockwise, carrying ladies of the ensemble in elegant gowns gliding past the two men like a rotating carousel of a ballroom dance floor.
- The movement is smooth, stylized, and rapid-fire, using the turntable to create a seamless visual time-jump into the party atmosphere without pausing the music.
- At the very end of the track, the turntable tracks lock into place, centering Hamilton as Eliza enters the space for the next song.
