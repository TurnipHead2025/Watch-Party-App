---
Title: 23 - Non-Stop
Set Layout: The ultimate structural culmination of Act 1. Every part of the set is utilized: the upper scaffolding represents balconies, courtrooms, and ship decks, while portable desks and wooden chairs spin in and out of the center.
Lighting: Hyper-dynamic, rapidly shifting color palettes. Brilliantly bright white courtroom spots quickly slam down, transitioning into warm amber party washes, and finally exploding into a massive, full-stage golden-hour wash for the finale chorus.
Blocking Elements:
- Hamilton is the relentless visual anchor, pacing furiously downstage center or sitting at a moving desk while the world accelerates around him.
- The dual-ring turntables operate at peak capacity: the outer ring spins clockwise carrying the ensemble in sharp, frantic daily-life choreography, while the inner ring carries Burr, Angelica, Eliza, and Washington in counter-rotating paths around Hamilton.
- In the final counterpoint section, the entire cast populates the multi-level scaffolding, leaning over the rails to surround Hamilton physically and sonically.
- The act ends with Hamilton climbing to the highest central platform of the scaffolding, stepping forward into a brilliant white spotlight with a sheet of paper raised high, frozen in absolute defiance as the lights instantly slam to black.
