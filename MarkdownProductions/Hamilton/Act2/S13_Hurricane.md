Title: 36 - Hurricane
Set Layout: Completely bare, vast downstage arena. The rest of the stage fades completely into pitch blackness.
Lighting: A single, intense, overhead blinding white spotlight pinned directly on Hamilton at center stage. A swirling, hypnotic pattern of deep blue and violet pin-spots slowly rotates around him on the floor.
Blocking Elements:
- Hamilton stands perfectly still downstage center, completely suspended in the metaphorical eye of the storm, delivering his lines with a heavy, deeply internal gravitas.
- The ensemble moves in hyper-slow motion on the outer spinning turntable ring, lifting heavy wooden chairs, writing desks, and loose sheets of paper into the air around him.
- This slow-motion, floating choreography physically manifests the wreckage of his past, his memories, and the leaks of the incoming scandal swirling around his mind.
- The movement is eerie, fluid, and dreamlike, emphasizing his absolute psychological isolation before he decides to write the pamphlet.
