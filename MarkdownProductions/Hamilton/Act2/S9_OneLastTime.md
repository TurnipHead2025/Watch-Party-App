Title: 32 - One Last Time
Set Layout: A formal executive office. A single heavy wooden executive desk and chair are positioned stage right.
Lighting: Starts in cold, administrative steel-blue. As the song progresses into Washington's farewell address, the lights beautifully transition into a majestic, full-stage golden-hour amber wash.
Blocking Elements:
- George Washington begins the track seated at the desk stage right, his physical posture heavily weighed down by the burden of leadership.
- Hamilton stands slightly behind him to stage left, acting as his scribe, typing furiously at a small invisible desk in the shadows.
- Washington steps away from the desk, marching directly downstage center to deliver his final lines with an open, powerful, deeply vulnerable physical stance.
- The inner turntable rotates at a barely perceptible crawl under Washington's feet, slowly presenting him to the audience from all angles as he physically sheds his mantle of power.
