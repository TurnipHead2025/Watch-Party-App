Title: 38 - Burn
Set Layout: Complete domestic isolation. The stage floor is entirely cleared of all furniture, props, and ensemble members, leaving only a single small wooden chest or bench downstage center.
Lighting: A deep, heavy, saturated midnight-blue and amber wash. A tight, flickering, warm orange spotlight pins directly down on Eliza, simulating the raw glow of a fireplace.
Blocking Elements:
- Eliza is the absolute emotional and physical anchor of the stage, standing or kneeling downstage center next to the wooden chest.
- She holds real paper letters, her movements slow, deliberate, and deeply grounded.
- As she sings, she holds a letter over a small, hidden amber light source inside the chest to physically simulate burning the correspondence, casting an eerie, flickering fire-glow onto her face.
- The dual-ring turntables are completely locked and stationary, emphasizing her profound grief and the sudden, devastating halt of her world.
---
