Set Layout: A bustling, youthful New York City street transitioning into a tense dueling ground (Weehawken, New Jersey). 
Lighting: Shifts from a bright, optimistic, sunny afternoon gold wash to a stark, low-angle, chilly steel-blue dusk light at the end of the track.
Blocking Elements:
- Philip Hamilton owns the downstage center space, entering with a bouncy, cocky, high-stepping energy that directly mirrors his father's youthful Act 1 choreography.

- George Eacker stands rigid and towering upstage left on the outer ring, completely ignored by Philip until they cross paths downstage center for their heated confrontation.
- At the very end of the track, the music drops out, the turntables lock instantly, and a cold, narrow white spotlight snaps down on Philip and his second as they prepare the pistols.
