Title: 40 - Stay Alive (Reprise)
Set Layout: A chaotic, sterile hospital room. A single heavy wooden table or makeshift medical bed is rolled out to downstage center.
Lighting: A cold, clinical, high-contrast white overhead spotlight harsh on the bed, while the rest of the stage is cast in a deep, sorrowful indigo shadow.
Blocking Elements:
- Philip Hamilton lies wounded on the central bed, his physical movement completely stilled.
- Alexander and Eliza rush from opposite wings, meeting downstage center to collapse over the bed, holding his hands from either side.
- The dual-ring turntables are completely locked and stationary, freezing the physical space as the family shares their final, quiet counting song.
- On the final count, the overhead light slams to a stark, cold blue silhouette as the ensemble enters from the shadows to remove the bed.
