---
Title: 46 - Finale (Who Lives, Who Dies, Who Tells Your Story)
Set Layout: The complete structural unity of the show. The multi-level wooden scaffolding is fully populated by the entire cast looking down at the stage floor.
Lighting: Soft midnight blue ambient background. A single, pure, brilliant white spotlight illuminates Eliza downstage center, casting her silhouette upward against the back wall.
Blocking Elements:
- Washington, Jefferson, Madison, and Burr step forward sequentially on the outer turntable ring to deliver their introductory lines before retreating back to the ensemble.
- Eliza enters from upstage center, walking down downstage to take her place as the absolute visual and emotional anchor of the finale.
- The entire cast surrounds her on the stage floor and catwalks, standing completely static to let her voice dominate the space.
- On her final breath, Eliza steps directly to the lip of the stage, looks up at the balcony lights, and lets out a breathless gasp as the entire stage instantly cuts to absolute black.
