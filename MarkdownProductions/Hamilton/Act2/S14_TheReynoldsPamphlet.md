Title: 37 - The Reynolds Pamphlet
Set Layout: Full-stage open clearance. The multi-level wooden scaffolding is heavily active, with ensemble members leaning over the rails to mock Hamilton.
Lighting: High-contrast, frantic, rapid-fire white strobe lights mixed with intense, saturated crimson-red bars cutting horizontally across the stage.
Blocking Elements:
- The ensemble breaks out into chaotic, mocking, high-energy modern choreography on the spinning turntables, throwing physical papers wildly into the air around Hamilton.
- Jefferson, Madison, and Burr stand downstage left, gleefully handing out pamphlets to the audience and the ensemble, completely vindicated.
- Hamilton stands frozen downstage center, physically bombarded by the papers, his posture completely broken.
- The frantic visual mania abruptly cuts to a stark, dead-silent, ice-cold blue silhouette the exact moment Eliza steps onto the upper catwalk stage right.
