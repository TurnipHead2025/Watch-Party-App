Title: 28 - The Room Where It Happens
Set Layout: A private dining room. A single central wooden dining table with three chairs occupies the middle of the floor.
Lighting: Starts in low-intensity, secretive green and deep teal spotlights casting elongated shadows. On the climatic shift ("I wanna be in the room!"), the stage explodes into flashing, high-contrast white and bright gold showstopper lighting.
Blocking Elements:
- Aaron Burr is the frantic, unhinged narrator, initially circling the outer perimeter of the stage on the moving turntable ring while tracking the meeting from a distance.
- Hamilton, Jefferson, and Madison occupy the inner turntable ring, their movements locked in sync with the rotating table furniture as they toast their secret deal.
- The ensemble acts as silent, faceless waiters in matching waistcoats, sliding props and chairs flawlessly across the spinning rings to accentuate Burr's exclusion.
- At the climax, Burr leaps directly onto the center of the dining table, executing explosive, high-energy jazz choreography with his cane as the ensemble surrounds the table in a massive, synchronized kick-line.
